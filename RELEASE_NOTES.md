## v0.5.17 — First release

First release of Bornomala 🎉

• Bangla typing with Avro phonetic — spell it the way it sounds.
• Clean English QWERTY with long-press symbols & accents.
• Fully offline. No internet, no tracking, no data collected.
• Emoji & clipboard panels, word suggestions, language switch.
• Themes, adjustable size, fonts, and a live "Try now" preview.

Thanks for trying it — feedback welcome!
