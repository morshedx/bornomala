Bornomala is a fast, private keyboard for Bangla and English — built for people who care about how they type and who has their data. The answer: nobody. Everything stays on your phone.

Type Bangla the natural way with Avro phonetic transliteration. Spell words the way they sound in English letters and Bornomala renders proper Bangla — conjuncts, vowel signs, and all.

ami → আমি
bangladesh → বাংলাদেশ
kemon → কেমন

PRIVACY FIRST
• No internet permission — the app cannot send your data anywhere.
• No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts.
• Everything you type is processed on your device.
• The only permission used is optional vibration.

BANGLA, DONE RIGHT
• Avro phonetic typing with a real rule-based engine.
• Bengali digits and dari punctuation.
• Word suggestions, learned on-device.

ENGLISH TOO
• Clean QWERTY with shift, caps lock, number row, symbols.
• Long-press for symbols and accents, Gboard-style.
• Auto-capitalization and double-space-period.

MADE YOURS
• Light, dark, system, and rich color themes.
• Adjustable keyboard height, key gaps, and label size.
• Optional key borders and font choice.

EVERYDAY TOOLS
• Emoji keyboard with search, recents and frequents.
• Clipboard manager with history, pin and search — on-device.
